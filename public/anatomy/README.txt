The prepared figure retains the approved artist-authored form, source UVs and
baked muscle-fiber normal maps. Its exposed anatomy face is replaced by the
neutral head. Color assignment does not reshape the figure. Connected source
muscle pieces are mapped to 25 exposed training regions; the upper/middle/lower
chest, deltoid heads and trapezius subdivisions have illustrative soft color
boundaries. Spinal erectors and deep hip flexors use soft bilateral schematic
color projections on existing surfaces, adding two visible training regions.
The lumbar zones also have shallow paired surface relief. The arms are angled
slightly outward, and the cut collar tapers under the fitted jaw and neck.
The vertex deformation preserves the original triangle topology and UVs.
The visible serratus slips have their own region. Light tendon areas within assigned muscle pieces
receive the same training tint to make the surfaces visually continuous.
The base of the replacement neck receives a short, soft bilateral upper-trapezius
color continuation across the former hard clipping seam. This is schematic,
not dissected anatomy; upper neck, face, anterior neck, midline and skull remain neutral.
Unassigned support structures remain neutral in training mode.

Geometry is normalized to 1.88 units tall, Y up, anterior +Z, feet at zero.
The neck is clipped at 1.615 units. Textures are resized to at most 1024 pixels;
geometry uses EXT_meshopt_compression with high-precision attribute rounding.
No body triangle simplification is applied. Finger/foot geometry and muscle
surface detail remain part of the original artist's sculpt.
diego-manifest.json records provenance, palette IDs, checksums and precision.
scripts/build-diego-anatomy.mjs reproduces the prepared model from source.

fallback-front.png and fallback-back.png are neutral renders of this adapted
figure, CC BY 4.0 with the separate MakeHuman CC0 contribution retained.

These notices apply to the anatomy/model/render assets. Personal training data
are not part of the assets. Training colors are estimates from logged sessions,
not measured activation or medical recovery measurements. This is an illustrative
training visualization, not a clinically validated anatomical reconstruction.
